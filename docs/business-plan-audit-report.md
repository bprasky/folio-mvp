# Business Plan Audit Report
## AUDIT-ONLY MODE - No Changes Applied

This audit flags language that misaligns with Folio's current product reality: passive signal capture, pre-onboarding intelligence, and infrastructure positioning.

---

## 🔍 CATEGORY 1: Language Implying Value Only After Opt-In/Onboarding

### Issue #1.1: Executive Summary - Vendor Attribution Framing
**Location:** Line 18
**Quote:**
> "Vendors finally see who is specifying their products and how they perform across the market."

**Problem:** The word "finally" implies vendors gain visibility only after joining Folio. This understates that Folio already captures product signals through designer behavior before vendors opt in. The intelligence exists; vendors just need to claim it.

**Suggested Adjustment:**
> "Vendors gain visibility into who is specifying their products and how they perform across the market—intelligence that Folio has already captured through designer behavior."

### Issue #1.2: Vendor Workflow Section - "Vendors Join" Language
**Location:** Lines 1933-1943
**Quote:**
> "Vendors join the platform through:
> A. Product Tag Notifications
> 'You were tagged in a project on Folio.'
> This is the single highest-converting vendor acquisition strategy. 
> It is organic, inbound, and emotionally compelling:
> - designers are already using your products
> - you can see who they are
> - you can receive more attribution
> - you can upload your catalog
> - you can promote your SKU
> This creates exponential vendor adoption."

**Problem:** This frames vendor value as beginning at the moment of joining. It doesn't acknowledge that Folio already knows which designers are using which products before the vendor receives the notification. The intelligence precedes the opt-in.

**Suggested Adjustment:**
> "Vendors discover their existing presence on Folio through:
> A. Product Tag Notifications
> 'You were tagged in a project on Folio.'
> This is the single highest-converting vendor acquisition strategy because it reveals intelligence Folio has already captured. It is organic, inbound, and emotionally compelling:
> - designers are already using your products (Folio already knows this)
> - you can see who they are (this data exists before you join)
> - you can claim attribution for existing activity
> - you can upload your catalog to expand visibility
> - you can promote your SKU
> This creates exponential vendor adoption because vendors are discovering, not creating, their market presence."

## 🔍 CATEGORY 2: Framing as "Platform/Tool/Marketplace" vs "Infrastructure/Network/System"

### Issue #2.1: Executive Summary - "Platform" Overuse
**Location:** Lines 12, 18, 20, 24
**Quotes:**
- Line 12: "positioning Folio as a category-defining platform"
- Line 18: "unified platform that connects"
- Line 20: "No platform currently integrates"
- Line 24: "the definitive platform where design work starts"

**Problem:** While the document does use "infrastructure" in some places (line 24: "creative infrastructure"), it defaults to "platform" throughout, which implies a tool users opt into rather than infrastructure they depend on. The word "platform" appears 50+ times, while "infrastructure" appears only 3-4 times.

**Suggested Adjustment (selective):**
- Line 12: "positioning Folio as category-defining infrastructure"
- Line 18: "unified system that connects" (or "network")
- Line 20: "No system currently integrates"
- Line 24: Keep "infrastructure" (already correct)

**Note:** Not every instance needs changing, but key positioning statements should favor infrastructure/network/system language.

## 🔍 CATEGORY 5: Inconsistencies Between Designer Value (Immediate/Passive) and Vendor Value (Delayed/Explicit)

### Issue #5.1: Value Timing Inconsistency
**Location:** Throughout document
**Problem:** The document describes designer value as immediate and passive (they use Folio, value accrues automatically), but vendor value is described as requiring explicit onboarding, uploads, and participation. This creates an inconsistency—if designers are passively creating intelligence, vendors should be able to access that intelligence passively too (even if they can't claim it until they join).

**Suggested Adjustment:** Add language throughout vendor sections clarifying that intelligence exists before vendor participation; vendors are claiming access to existing data, not triggering its creation.
