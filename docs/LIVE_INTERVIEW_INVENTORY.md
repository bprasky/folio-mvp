Other implemented paths: legacy posting text/transcription/realtime interviews save project evidence, and recorded-interview upload/process/highlight approval uses `src/lib/interviews/`. Free-text article-topic contributions (`/api/contributions/[topicId]`) are submissions with topic eligibility, not a question interview, so they are not advertised as a live interview type. The exported older bio-chat and layered-org modal have no discovered app consumer; their current equivalents are the entries above.

## Shared boundaries

`live-interview/adapter-types.ts` is the client-safe contract. `adapters.ts` is server-only. The lifecycle persists its full private snapshot, stable receipt and raw transcription; the browser never supplies that authority. Canonical limits reject, never truncate, an answer; recovery keeps the complete live source. Controls never become quotations. Native completion remains native; call finish on fixed-question sets means stopping with saved partial progress, not marking editorial coverage or approval complete.

Native guided/Spotlight/expertise services retain their transactions/receipts. Bio, org and collaboration use shared extracted writers for both text and live, with ownership rechecks and optimistic digests. No model call runs inside their write transactions. `resolveMediaSource` reauthorizes and rechecks source version/public eligibility on media delivery.

Verification: `node scripts/check-live-adapters.cjs` exercises adapter dispatch, canonical identity, retry/control handling and actual bio/org/collaboration writers against the repository's isolated schema-aware database double. This is not PostgreSQL lock/isolation or live-provider proof. Existing guided, Spotlight, expertise and story persistence suites cover their native engines separately. Feature enablement, paid provider smoke tests, private storage deployment and migration application remain deployment checks, not implied by source inspection.
