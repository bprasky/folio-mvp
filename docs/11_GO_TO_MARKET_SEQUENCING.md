# 11_GO_TO_MARKET_SEQUENCING (REVISED)

## Purpose
This document defines Folio's phased go-to-market strategy, showing how we move from initial wedge to full ecosystem systematically.

**Revision Note:** This version incorporates commerce activation into Phase 1, reflecting the strategic decision to prove the economic loop alongside the publishing workflow.

---

## The Wedge Strategy

Folio's vision is to become the operating system of interior design.
But we're not building everything at once.

We're starting with a wedge that proves **both** the content value **and** the economic model: **Designer publishing + Product attribution + Commerce activation**

## Why This Revised Wedge?

1. **Immediate designer value** - Better portfolio than Instagram grid
2. **Instant distribution** - Instagram becomes our marketing channel
3. **Economic proof** - Trade discount arbitrage validates business model
4. **Attribution from day one** - Vendors see value before they even join
5. **Designer earning** - Real money (as credit) from past work
6. **Data foundation** - Product tags + purchases power Atelier intelligence

---

## The Expansion Path

```
Phase 1A: Designer Publishing (Months 1-3)
    ↓
Phase 1B: Commerce Activation (Months 3-6)
    ↓
Phase 1C: Floodgates / Instagram Push (Month 6)
    ↓
Phase 2: Workflow Integration (Months 6-12)
    ↓
Phase 3: Vendor Scale (Months 12-18)
    ↓
Phase 4: Full Marketplace (Year 2+)
```

Each phase unlocks the next. We don't move forward until the current phase is validated.

---

## Phase 1A: Designer Publishing Foundation (Months 1-3)

**Objective:** Onboard founding designers, populate platform with real project content, generate the product tag dataset that becomes vendor outreach leverage.

**What we're building:**
- Designer portfolio pages
- Project post creation with editorial layouts
- AI-powered layout generation from images
- Product tagging workflow (AI-assisted, ~40 tags per designer)
- Basic engagement tracking (saves, likes, views)
- Simple discovery feed

**What we're NOT doing yet:**
- Instagram cross-posting push (holding this)
- Vendor outreach (building the leverage first)
- Commerce/checkout flows
- Public marketing

**Success metrics:**
- 5-10 founding designers onboarded (free, white-glove)
- 200-400 product tags across their projects
- Editorial AI quality validated by designer feedback
- Product tag dataset ready for vendor outreach

**Go-to-market tactics:**
- Direct outreach to target designers (1M+ cumulative Instagram reach)
- "Founding designer" early access with free premium forever
- White-glove onboarding support
- Quiet phase — no public launch

---

## Phase 1B: Commerce Activation (Months 3-6)

**Objective:** Use the product tag dataset to recruit vendors, configure trade accounts, and prove the commerce loop works.

**What we're building:**
- Vendor trade account configuration (login, quote, rep-mediated methods)
- Folio Credit system (non-cash, trade-redeemable)
- Order flow with attribution tracking
- Designer credit earning on attributed purchases
- Rep attribution and credit earning
- Monthly credit availability windows (30-day hold)
- Basic treasury management

**Vendor recruitment approach:**

Product tags from Phase 1A become outreach leverage:

> "Your products are already being specified on Folio. [Designer Name] tagged [Product] in their published project. Claim your attribution — we'll show you which designers are driving interest in your products."

**Target: 15-20 vendors minimum** with configured trade accounts before Phase 1C.

**Success metrics:**
- 15-20 vendors with trade accounts active
- First 10 orders processed through platform
- Designer credit earn/redeem cycle tested
- Rep attribution configured for participating vendors
- Returns flow validated

**Go-to-market tactics:**
- Data-driven vendor outreach (tagged products = warm leads)
- White-glove vendor onboarding
- Explain trade discount arbitrage model
- Position rep attribution as vendor benefit (visibility into rep effectiveness)

---

## Phase 1C: Floodgates / Instagram Push (Month 6)

**Objective:** With commerce infrastructure proven and 15-20 vendors live, release Instagram cross-posting and tell founding designers to go public.

**What happens:**
- Instagram cross-posting enabled for all founding designers
- Founding designers post to Instagram with Folio attribution
- Combined reach: 1M+ followers see Folio content
- FOMO drives designer applications and vendor inquiries

**The psychology:**
- Other designers see founding designers publishing through Folio
- Vendors see their competitors' products tagged and attributed
- The platform is visibly "live" with real designers, real vendors, real commerce
- Can't be worked around without contributing

**Success metrics:**
- 50%+ of founding designer Instagram posts originate from Folio
- Measurable traffic from Instagram → Folio
- Inbound vendor inquiries from Instagram exposure
- Designer waitlist building
- First purchases attributed to Instagram-discovered products

## Phase 2: Workflow Integration (Months 6-12)

**What we're building:**

- Designer credit redemption on client orders occurring

**Go-to-market tactics:**
- Case studies from founding designers (including earnings)
- "I earned $X from my past projects" testimonials
- Workflow webinars and demos
- Expansion beyond founding cohort

## Current Focus: Phase 1A Execution

### Founding Designer Selection Criteria

- Active on Instagram (proves they value sharing)
- Portfolio of completed projects (content to tag)
- 50K+ Instagram followers preferred (cumulative 1M+ reach target)
- Diverse aesthetics (test AI across styles)
- Willing to give detailed feedback
- Professional practice (not students/hobbyists)

### What We're Offering Founding Designers

- **Founding designer status** and recognition forever
- **Free premium features** during beta and beyond
- **Folio Credit earning** from day one (once commerce activates)
- **Direct influence** on product direction
- **White-glove onboarding** support
- **Priority for editorial features**

### The Ask

- Publish 2-4 completed projects on Folio
- Tag products in their images (~40 tags total)
- Provide honest feedback on workflows
- Participate in monthly feedback sessions
- **Hold Instagram posting until Phase 1C** (coordinate the push)

### Why Designers Should Say Yes

> "Tag the products you've already specified. When commerce activates, you'll earn Folio Credit on every purchase attributed to your work. That credit is spendable at full trade price when you place client orders. Your past projects become a tax-advantaged income stream."

## Vendor Outreach Strategy (Phase 1B)

### The "You Were Tagged" Email

```
Subject: [Designer Name] tagged your product on Folio

[Vendor Name],

[Designer Name] just published a project on Folio featuring your [Product Name].

Folio is a new platform where designers publish their work with full product attribution. When a product is tagged, you can:

- See which designers are specifying your products
- Understand which aesthetics align with your brand
- Track how designer influence converts to purchases

[Designer Name] has [X followers] on Instagram and their Folio project has already received [Y saves].

Claim your vendor profile to see your full attribution data:
[Link]

— Folio Team
```

### Vendor Pitch Meeting Flow

1. Show them their products already tagged (they're playing catch-up)
2. Explain attribution data they'll receive (designer fingerprints, aesthetic alignment)
3. Present trade discount arbitrage model (how purchases work)
4. Explain rep attribution (turns their field team into Folio advocates)
5. Describe Folio Credit (designers earning, reps earning, ecosystem retention)
6. Offer founding vendor benefits (featured placement, priority support)

### Vendor Qualifying Criteria

- Products appear in founding designer tags (warm leads)
- Trade account structure accessible (login, quote, or rep-mediated)
- Discount percentage known (need to model split)
- Willingness to configure rep attribution (bonus, not required)

## Risk Mitigation

### Phase 1A Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Designers don't see value vs. Instagram | White-glove support, founding status, future credit earning |
| AI layouts aren't good enough | Manual override, designer editing tools |
| Product tagging feels like work | AI-assisted tagging, clear economic payoff coming |
| Founders leak to Instagram early | Clear communication about coordinated push |

### Phase 1B Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Vendors don't respond to outreach | Lead with attribution data they can't get elsewhere |
| Trade accounts too complex to configure | Focus on quote-based flow first, expand to logins |
| Can't reach 15 vendor threshold | Lower threshold or extend timeline, don't launch 1C prematurely |
| Rep attribution rejected | Make it optional, prove model without it first |

### Phase 1C Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Instagram algorithm buries Folio posts | Multiple designers posting, cross-promotion |
| Competitors copy immediately | Intelligence moat from tagged data + credit system stickiness |
| Commerce can't handle volume | Test thoroughly in 1B, have manual fallback |
| Designer coordination fails | Clear launch day, group communication |

## Success Definition: End of Phase 1

By month 6, we have:

1. **5-10 founding designers** actively publishing with 200-400 product tags
2. **15-20 vendors** with configured trade accounts
3. **Commerce loop proven** with first 10+ orders processed
4. **Designer credit** earning and redemption both tested
5. **Instagram push executed** with measurable traffic
6. **Inbound demand** from both designers and vendors
7. **Folio Credit system** operational with monthly windows

This foundation enables Phase 2 expansion with proven economics, not assumptions.

---

## Why This Sequencing Works

1. **Prove economics early** - Don't wait 12 months to validate the business model
2. **Leverage before outreach** - Vendors respond to "your products are already tagged"
3. **Coordinated launch** - Instagram push has maximum impact with full roster
4. **Designer incentive alignment** - Earning potential from day one
5. **Capital efficient** - Prove commerce with 15-20 vendors, not 200
6. **Reversible decisions** - Can adjust split percentages, vendor terms based on learning

---

## Conclusion

The revised Phase 1 strategy integrates commerce activation into the initial wedge rather than deferring it. This:

- Proves the economic model alongside the content model
- Creates immediate earning potential for designers
- Generates vendor outreach leverage through attribution data
- Enables a coordinated Instagram push with commerce infrastructure ready

Folio isn't just launching a publishing platform. Folio is launching the economic infrastructure that makes designer influence capturable and monetizable.

The floodgates open when the system is ready to capture the flood.
