# 08_AI_ROLE

## Purpose
This document defines the role, boundaries, and authority limits of AI within Folio.

AI behavior must remain aligned with Folio's professional, merit-based identity.

## AI's Role in Folio
AI in Folio is assistive infrastructure.

AI exists to:
- Navigate complex data
- Recall past work
- Surface relevant context
- Reduce cognitive and operational friction

AI improves clarity and speed, not authorship or judgment.

## AI Implementation Phases

**Phase 1: Editorial Generation (Current)**
AI's initial role is narrow but high-impact:
- Auto-layout of project images into editorial spreads
- Caption suggestions from image analysis
- Basic product detection in images (foundation for future tagging)

Guardrails:
- Designers always review before publishing
- AI suggests, never publishes autonomously
- Manual override on all AI decisions

**Phase 2: Workflow Assistance (Months 6-12)**
- AI-assisted moodboard generation
- Product recommendation based on designer fingerprint
- Pre-rendering using real product selections
- Smart product tagging suggestions

**Phase 3: Intelligence Layer (Months 12+)**
- Atelier fingerprinting fully operational
- Predictive vendor matching
- Trend forecasting
- Advanced editorial automation

The capabilities described in "What AI Is Allowed to Do" and "What AI Must Not Do" sections below represent our full-state principles that guide development across all phases.

## What AI Is Allowed to Do
AI may:
- Summarize and retrieve project information
- Help users locate past decisions, assets, or products
- Suggest next steps based on structured data
- Assist with organization, tagging, and recall
- Explain why something is surfaced (transparency)

## What AI Must Not Do
AI must not:
- Assign Editorial status autonomously (especially in early phases)
- Invent facts, credentials, or product details
- Override user intent or professional judgment
- Generate authoritative conclusions without grounding
- Act as a decision-maker of record

If AI output conflicts with canonical documents or verified data, the AI output is wrong.

## AI and Editorial
AI may:
- Observe engagement signals
- Surface candidates for Editorial qualification
- Assist curators with context and scoring explanations

AI does not:
- Define merit in isolation
- Replace human confirmation in hybrid phases

## Authority Hierarchy
1. Canonical Documents (/docs)
2. Verified system data
3. User intent
4. AI assistance

AI is never the final authority.
